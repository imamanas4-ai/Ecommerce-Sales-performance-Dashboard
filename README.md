# E-Commerce Sales Performance & Customer Analytics

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard analysing e-commerce sales, customer satisfaction, delivery performance, geographic trends, product performance, and revenue concentration.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyse e-commerce sales data and identify important business patterns across revenue, customers, products, delivery performance, customer satisfaction, and geographic regions.

The analysis goes beyond visualisation by using KPIs, drill-down analysis, and business-focused insights to understand **what is happening, where it is happening, and what actions the business could take.**

## 🔎 Key Analytical Questions

The analysis was designed around the following business questions:

1. How much revenue and how many orders are being generated?
2. What is the average value of each order?
3. Which months contribute most to overall revenue?
4. Which product categories perform the best?
5. Which geographic regions contribute most to sales?
6. How satisfied are customers based on their ratings?
7. How efficient is the delivery process?
8. Are there areas where revenue is highly concentrated?
9. What business areas require further investigation?

---

## 🧠 Business Insights

The analysis highlights several areas that could influence business performance:

- Revenue is not evenly distributed across all periods.
- Certain product categories contribute more strongly to overall performance.
- Customer satisfaction provides an important measure of the overall customer experience.
- Delivery performance requires attention, with only 16% of orders classified as on-time.
- Geographic analysis can help identify high-performing regions and potential expansion opportunities.
- Revenue concentration should be monitored to reduce excessive dependence on a particular period or segment.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

### Improve Delivery Performance
Investigate the causes of delivery delays by analysing shipping methods, locations, processing times, and fulfilment performance.

### Investigate Revenue Concentration
Analyse high-revenue periods in greater detail to understand the products, customers, and regions responsible for the concentration.

### Strengthen High-Performing Categories
Identify successful product categories and investigate opportunities to increase their sales through better inventory planning, promotions, and customer targeting.

### Improve Customer Experience
Use customer ratings and delivery performance together to identify whether operational issues are affecting customer satisfaction.

### Use Geographic Insights for Growth
Focus marketing and expansion strategies on high-performing regions while investigating why other regions generate lower sales.

## 🔬 Analytical Approach

The project followed a practical data analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → PivotTables → KPI Analysis → Dashboard → Drill-Down Analysis → Business Insights → Recommendations**

### Data Cleaning & Transformation

Power Query was used to prepare and transform the data before analysis.

Key activities included:

- Removing duplicates
- Cleaning text fields
- Standardising data
- Transforming columns
- Reshaping data where required
- Preparing the dataset for analysis

### Data Analysis

PivotTables, calculated measures, and DAX were used to calculate and investigate:

- Total orders
- Total revenue
- Average Order Value
- Customer ratings
- Delivery performance
- Product/category performance
- Geographic performance
- Revenue concentration
